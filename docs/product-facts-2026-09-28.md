## Recommended shared copy

- Free tier compact: “Try every available voice for your first 8 hours of listening. Afterwards, keep a free English voice selection and unlimited book listening. No LoudReader account or word quota.” If a page discusses choice, explain Stella/Rio plus Bella on supported devices.
- Private compact: “Speech is generated on your device; your books are not uploaded for narration.” Where privacy is the topic add diagnostics/analytics disclosure above.
- Imports compact: “Read DRM-free EPUBs, PDFs—including scans with on-device text recognition—and saved web articles.” Do not imply perfect OCR.
- Mac compact: “Runs on iPhone and iPad, and on Apple Silicon Macs as an iPad app.”
- Premium compact: “Unlock every available narrator, adjustable playback speed, sleep timer, soundscapes and unlimited article saving.” Add cloning with trial detail when relevant. Do not sell notes as an exclusive paid feature.
