**Format:** 16:9 horizontal

---

## Purpose

Address the objections before the viewer thinks of them: "Is this free?", "Do I need
an account?", "Will my data be safe?" Land all three in 7 seconds without slowing down.

---

## Visual Description

**Background:** Clean white or very light warm grey — neutral, professional.
No people. No environments. This is information delivery, not storytelling.

**Main visual:** LoudReader app screen recording plays in a centered phone mockup
(phone frame visible — iPhone, clean). The app flows through:
- Home screen → Library → Player with highlighting → Voice selection → Offline indicator

The recording is smooth and clearly produced — this is the "hero demo" moment.

---
