# Scene 02 — The Demonstration
**Ad:** Segment 2 — The Busy Commuter
**Timestamp:** 0:06–0:20
**Duration:** 14 seconds
**Format:** 16:9 horizontal
**Energy:** Clear, informative, satisfying — the "how" revealed

---

## Purpose

Show exactly how the product works without losing the viewer's attention.
The split-screen format connects product action to real-world context simultaneously —
you see what you press AND where you end up being able to listen.

---

## Visual Description

**Layout:** True split-screen. Clean vertical dividing line in the center of the 16:9 frame.
Left half: phone screen recording. Right half: real-life footage.

**Left side — Screen recording sequence (continuous, smooth):**
1. LoudReader home screen — warm beige, clean minimal UI
2. User taps "Import from Files"
3. Files app picker opens — a list of EPUB files visible
4. One tapped — a book cover animates large across the player screen
5. Large play button. Tap.
6. Text renders — line by line — and begins highlighting word by word in cyan (`#2EC7F6`)
7. Hold on highlighted text scrolling for 3 seconds — let viewers appreciate it

The recording should feel effortless. Import to listening in under 10 seconds shown.

**Right side — Real-life commuter:**
Person from Scene 01, now on the subway. Seated by window. AirPods in.
The phone is slightly visible in their hand or lap — matching the left side state.
Natural train motion — slight sway, scenery blurring past the window.
They look calm, slightly nodding, occasional eye movement reading words they're hearing.
