# Scene 01 — Hook (Must Survive 5-Second Skip)
**Ad:** Segment 2 — The Busy Commuter
**Timestamp:** 0:00–0:06
**Duration:** 6 seconds
**Format:** 16:9 horizontal
**Energy:** Slow, observational → suddenly charged

---

## Purpose

Beat the skip. The first 5 seconds must land the entire premise before the viewer
can press "Skip Ad." The key insight ("you already have 90 minutes a day") must hit
in the first sentence. The visual contrast between bored and engaged must be immediate.

---

## Visual Description

**Part A (0:00–0:03) — The Bored Commuter:**

Slow-motion. A young professional (late 20s, business casual — neat but not stiff)
standing alone on a subway platform. The platform is wide, slightly empty,
harsh overhead fluorescents. They're looking at their watch. Weight shifted to one hip.
Not miserable — just in that familiar commuter blank stare. Thousands of mornings
have looked exactly like this.

Camera: Static, wide-medium. The person centered. Platform stretching behind them
into soft focus. No one approaches them. They are alone with the routine.

**Part B (0:03–0:06) — Hard cut. Same person, same platform, same clothes:**

But now: AirPods in. Eyes slightly upward — thinking, processing, engaged.
A small, genuine smile. They're *somewhere else* in their mind. Present in their
body but traveling in a story. The platform is the same but they are different.

Camera: Slightly tighter — medium close-up. Same angle, but warmer.

---

## Lighting

Part A: Harsh platform fluorescents. Cold, slightly green-cast overhead light.
Slightly desaturated. The visual language of "another forgettable morning."

Part B: Same overhead lighting, but this time it feels more neutral — warmer grade
applied in color correction. Same physical light, different emotional register.
