# Scene 03 — Day in the Life
**Ad:** Segment 2 — The Busy Commuter
**Timestamp:** 0:20–0:35
**Duration:** 15 seconds total (4 shots × ~3–4 seconds each)
**Format:** 16:9 horizontal
**Energy:** Warm, aspirational, cinematic — the life they want

---

## Purpose

Expand the use case beyond commuting. Show that LoudReader isn't just for trains —
it fills every gap in a modern professional day. Each timestamp anchors the
narrative in real, recognizable moments.

---

## Shot A — Morning Coffee (0:20–0:24)

**Visual:** Kitchen counter. A beautiful pour-over coffee or espresso in frame.
Steam rising. Phone propped against a coffee cup or small stand on the counter —
the LoudReader app visible, text scrolling and word highlighting in cyan.
Person visible in the background, getting ready — slightly out of focus,
in a suit jacket or smart-casual. Moving around, checking their bag, not looking
at the phone. Listening passively.

**Camera:** Close-medium. Static or very slow push toward the phone and coffee.
The focus pulls from coffee steam to the phone screen.

**Lighting:** Early morning kitchen — soft grey pre-dawn light through window,
warm pendant lights above. That specific early-morning-haven't-quite-started-yet quality.

**Timestamp overlay:** `7:14 AM` — top-left, small, clean, white.

**Seedance prompt:**
> Cinematic 16:9 kitchen close-up. Pour-over coffee or espresso on a modern kitchen counter, steam rising. A smartphone is propped against the cup showing a reading/listening app with cyan text highlighting. In the background, a person in smart-casual clothes is getting ready — blurred, in motion. Early morning light: cool grey through window + warm pendant kitchen lights. Static camera or slow push toward screen. Aspirational domestic morning. Shallow depth of field. Photorealistic. Film grain. Timestamp "7:14 AM" top-left (add in post).

## Shot B — Subway Commute (0:24–0:27)

**Visual:** Underground subway, moving. Window from inside the carriage.
The person is visible in the window reflection — AirPods in, eyes forward.
Outside: dark tunnel walls, the rhythmic flash of lights as the train speeds through.
Or: a surface commute with city streets blurring past.

The phone is barely visible — just the faint glow of the app in their hand.
We're meant to infer they're listening, not see them demonstrating it.

**Camera:** Over-the-shoulder. Person and window ahead. Slight sway with the train.

**Lighting:** Mix of dim carriage interior light and rhythmic external tunnel lights.
Slightly moody but not dark.

**Timestamp overlay:** `8:30 AM` — top-left.

**Seedance prompt:**
> Cinematic 16:9, inside a moving subway carriage. Over-the-shoulder shot of a young professional looking ahead. Dark tunnel walls outside the window, rhythmic light flashes as train moves through. AirPods visible. Phone barely visible with faint screen glow. Carriage interior ambient lighting, slight motion sway. Mysterious, slightly cinematic. Shallow depth of field. Photorealistic. Film grain. Timestamp "8:30 AM" add in post.

standing on the table, app playing. They're eating relaxed, looking slightly upward
or out, thinking about what they're hearing. Earbuds in. Unbothered. This is the
best lunch break they've had in a while.

**Camera:** Medium wide. The person slightly off-center. Park trees / café blurred
background. Bright midday natural light.

**Lighting:** Bright midday sun, slightly soft (clouds or outdoor shade). Natural, clean, warm.

**Timestamp overlay:** `12:45 PM` — top-left.

Park trees or café decor softly blurred in background. Medium wide shot, person slightly off-center. Warm, peaceful. Photorealistic. Film grain. Timestamp "12:45 PM" add in post.

## Shot D — Evening / Sleep Timer (0:31–0:35)

**Visual:** Person in bed, evening. Warm golden bedside lamp light.
Phone on the pillow next to their head. The screen shows the LoudReader sleep timer UI —
a countdown circle, with "15:00" counting down. Soft. The AI narrator is audible
just barely in the audio bed.

Their eyes are heavy but content. They're listening to the last chapter. Slowly,
the screen dims automatically (sleep timer reaching end). Their eyes close. Small
exhale. Peaceful.

**Camera:** Low angle, close to pillow height. Intimate, quiet. The phone screen
provides warm light on their face.

**Lighting:** Warm amber bedside lamp. Very intimate, golden. Almost candlelight quality.

**Text overlay:** `Falls asleep on its own.` — appears as their eyes close, fades out.
Style: white, elegant, small. Space Grotesk Regular.

**Seedance prompt:**
> Cinematic 16:9. Intimate low-angle shot, close to pillow level. Person lying in bed in a cozy bedroom, eyes growing heavy but with a content expression. Smartphone on the pillow beside them, screen visible with a countdown timer interface. Warm golden amber light from bedside lamp, almost candlelight quality. Very gentle, intimate mood. Screen glow provides warm light on their face. The scene feels like the end of a perfect day. Shallow depth of field. Photorealistic. Film grain.
