# Scene 02 — The Shift (Workout Montage)
**Ad:** Segment 9 — The Fitness Optimizer
**Timestamp:** 0:04–0:12
**Duration:** 8 seconds (4 cuts × ~2 seconds each)
**Format:** 9:16 vertical
**Energy:** High energy, kinetic, aspirational — the upgraded workout

---

## Purpose

Show the same workout moments from Scene 01's implied "wasted" time —
but now transformed by LoudReader. Each shot is a workout moment made richer.
The montage should feel aspirational — this is what their routine could feel like.

---

## Visual Description

Four shots, each 2 seconds.

**Cut A — Treadmill:**
Person running on a treadmill, strong pace, AirPods in.
LoudReader visible on their phone in a treadmill mount or phone holder on the machine.
They look focused but absorbed — not just enduring the run. Their face shows they're
thinking about what they're hearing.

Camera: Medium shot from the side, slightly low angle — dynamic, shows the motion.

Lighting: Modern gym — bright overhead + natural window light if available. Clean, energetic.

**Cut B — Dumbbell curls:**
Person doing dumbbell bicep curls in a gym mirror. AirPods in.
Expression: present, engaged, slightly nodding to a point they just heard.
The workout and the listening are coexisting effortlessly.

Camera: Front-facing, medium — shows face and upper body, dumbbells in motion.

Lighting: Gym mirror with overhead lighting. Slight lens flare acceptable.

**Cut C — Outdoor morning run:**
Pre-dawn or golden hour. Empty road or park path. Person running steadily.
AirPods in. The air is fresh. This is the best kind of run — unhurried, mindful.

Camera: Reverse tracking (camera ahead of them, moving backward as they approach).
We see their face — calm, somewhere deep in a book.

Lighting: Golden hour / blue hour. Beautiful.

**Cut D — Exercise bike / cycling:**
Person on a stationary bike or cycling trainer at home. Slightly less intense pace —
this is zone 2 cardio, the perfect mindless-body/active-mind state.
AirPods in. Phone visible on a small stand beside the bike, app running.
They're clearly tuned in to what they're hearing.

Camera: Medium, slightly overhead angle.

Lighting: Home or gym — warm ambient.
