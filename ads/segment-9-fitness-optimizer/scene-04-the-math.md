# Scene 04 — The Math
**Ad:** Segment 9 — The Fitness Optimizer
**Timestamp:** 0:18–0:22
**Duration:** 4 seconds
**Format:** 9:16 vertical
**Energy:** Sharp, numbers-forward, slightly satisfying — the optimization payoff

---

## Purpose

Give this segment the calculation they can't resist. Fitness optimizers track
everything in numbers. Show them exactly what their workout time converts to in
book output — and make the number impressive enough to act on immediately.

---

## Visual Description

**Full-screen typography animation on dark background.**

Numbers build line by line — each appearing with a short delay:

```
5 hrs/week at the gym
+ 30 min walks daily
─────────────────────
~8 hrs of listening/week
```

*(pause)*

```
= 1 book every 10 days
```

*(pause — the big one)*

```
26 books a year.
From workouts alone.
```

The final two lines are significantly larger and bolder than the calculation above.
The conclusion is the payoff — it should feel like a personal record being announced.

---

## Typography Spec

- Calculation lines: Space Grotesk Regular, medium size, white
- "= 1 book every 10 days": Space Grotesk Bold, larger, white
- "26 books a year.": Space Grotesk Bold, very large, Loud Blue (#2EC7F6) or white
- "From workouts alone.": Space Grotesk Regular, medium, white at lower opacity

Dark background — the same near-black as Scene 01. Visual continuity bookends the ad.
