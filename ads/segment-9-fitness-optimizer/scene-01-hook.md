# Scene 01 — Hook (The Time Math)
**Ad:** Segment 9 — The Fitness Optimizer
**Timestamp:** 0:00–0:04
**Duration:** 4 seconds
**Format:** 9:16 vertical
**Energy:** Bold, punchy, slightly confrontational — the scroll-stopper

---

## Purpose

A single number — workout hours per week — reframed as wasted opportunity.
This audience tracks their workout time obsessively. Pointing it back at them
as "time you could be reading" is a pattern interrupt they can't scroll past.

---

## Visual Description

**Pure typography animation. No photography. Fills the 9:16 frame.**

Dark background (near-black or dark charcoal).

Line 1 appears first — large, bold:
```
You spent 5 hours at the gym this week.
```
*(pause 0.5s)*

Line 2 appears below — same size but slightly lower weight:
```
How many books did you finish?
```

*(pause 0.7s — let the question sit)*

Nothing else. The frame is held.

---

## Typography Spec

- Line 1: Space Grotesk Bold, white, approximately 36–40pt equivalent (fills ~70% of width)
- Line 2: Space Grotesk Regular or Medium, white at 85% opacity, slightly smaller
- Both centered horizontally, stacked with generous gap between them
- The contrast between Bold (the statement) and Regular (the question) is intentional
