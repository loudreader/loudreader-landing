## Target Audience

**Who they are:**
People who work out 4–6 times a week — gym, running, cycling, HIIT, walks.
They already listen to something during workouts (music, podcasts, Spotify).
They care about optimizing every part of their day. They track their macros,
their sleep, their steps. Books are on their "I want to do more of that" list
but they never seem to find the time.

**Demographics:** 22–38, any gender, health-conscious, high-achiever mindset.
Heavy TikTok + Instagram users. Follow fitness creators, productivity influencers.

**State of mind:** Already open to tools that help them optimize. They just haven't
made the mental link between "workout time" and "reading time." This ad makes that
link for them. Once they see it, it's obvious. The before/after is satisfying.

## Why This Segment Converts

1. **The time math is uniquely compelling** — someone who works out 5 hours a week
   and does a 20-minute walk twice daily has ~9 hours of "listening time" per week.
   At 1.5× speed, that's roughly one business book every 4–5 days. The math is shocking.
2. **They already listen during workouts** — the behavior is established. LoudReader
   is just a content upgrade from music/podcasts to books.
3. **Offline is critical** — gyms often have poor cell signal. Total offline function
   is a genuine differentiator over streaming audiobook services.
4. **Speed control** — at 1.5–2× they can get through more content during an
   intense session and keep up with faster thinking.
5. **No sync friction** — import your books, done. No subscription library to
   navigate during a rest set. One tap, playing.
