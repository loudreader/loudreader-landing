# Scene 03 — Product Tour
**Ad:** Segment 4 — The Privacy Rebel
**Timestamp:** 0:14–0:30
**Duration:** 16 seconds
**Format:** 16:9 horizontal
**Energy:** Informative, confident, real-world grounded

---

## Purpose

Show the product in action across environments where privacy matters most.
The split-screen format connects the technical proof (offline, no connection) to
real-world moments — this isn't a lab demo, it's someone's actual life.

---

## Visual Description

**Layout:** Split-screen (left: app states, right: real-world environments).
The dividing line is clean but minimal — a thin shadow or natural edge.

---

**Left Side — App flowing through states (continuous):**

1. Import screen — EPUB file list, no network activity shown
2. Offline indicator visible (wifi crossed out or airplane mode — playing anyway)
3. Player with cyan word-by-word highlighting
4. Voice selection screen — multiple AI voices listed
5. Settings screen (briefly) — shows "No account required", "Offline mode"

The flow is deliberate, not rushed. Let each state breathe for 2–3 seconds.

---

**Right Side — Three environment cuts:**

**Cut A (0:14–0:19) — Coffee shop:**
Person at a coffee shop table. Phone flat on the table, screen visible — app playing.
They're working on a laptop beside it. No earbuds — just AirPods. Relaxed.
The café wifi is visible on a menu sign behind them — they're not using it.
Natural café ambience. Warm midday light.

**Cut B (0:19–0:24) — Park:**
Person walking alone through a park path. Phone in hand, AirPods in. Trees around.
Zero signal indicator barely visible on phone screen corner. Still playing.
Leisurely pace — not a workout, just a walk. Thoughtful expression.

**Cut C (0:24–0:30) — Airplane:**
Window seat. Airplane mode icon visible top-right of phone screen. App still playing.
Clouds through the oval window. Headphones (over-ear or AirPods).
Person looks completely at ease — no anxiety about losing connection.
