# Scene 01 — The Accusation
**Ad:** Segment 4 — The Privacy Rebel
**Timestamp:** 0:00–0:06
**Duration:** 6 seconds
**Format:** 16:9 horizontal
**Energy:** Low, serious, slightly confrontational — a statement, not a scare

---

## Purpose

Call out the tracking reality that this segment already knows but rarely hears stated
plainly. The goal is not to shock — they already know this. The goal is to say
*"we see it too"* and establish instant trust through shared awareness.
Must survive the 5-second YouTube pre-roll skip.

---

## Visual Description

**Part A (0:00–0:04) — Rapid-fire text:**

Near-black background. One line of white text appears at a time, each holding for
~0.8 seconds before dissolving and being replaced by the next.

The text is not animating dramatically — it appears and disappears. Calm. Matter-of-fact.
This is more unsettling than if it were screaming.

Lines (in order):
1. `"Audible knows every book you've listened to."`
2. `"Spotify tracks every chapter."`
3. `"Your reading history is their data."`
4. `"When you stopped. Where you paused. What you skipped."`

Typography: Clean, minimal sans-serif. Left-aligned or centered. White on near-black.
Space Grotesk Regular. Not bold — the quietness is the point.

**Part B (0:04–0:06) — The reveal:**

Camera was slowly pulling back from the text screen the whole time, and now we see:
a person holding their phone, the text visible on the phone screen itself — they were
reading about this. They lower the phone slowly. Look toward camera.

Their expression is not shocked — it's the weary recognition of someone who already knew
and is tired of the status quo. Late 20s–early 40s. Could be a developer, a journalist,
a teacher. Smart-looking. Not paranoid — just principled.

Camera: Slow pull-back reveal. Medium shot by the end. The person stands in a slightly
dim, modern apartment or office space.
