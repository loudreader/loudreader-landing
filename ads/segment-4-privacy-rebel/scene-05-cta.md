# Scene 05 — CTA
**Ad:** Segment 4 — The Privacy Rebel
**Timestamp:** 0:40–0:45
**Duration:** 5 seconds
**Format:** 16:9 horizontal
**Energy:** Minimal, deliberate, silent — the anti-hype close

---

## Purpose

Deliberately subvert the usual loud CTA. This segment reacts against noise and
over-selling. A quiet, information-only close — with actual silence — signals that
LoudReader is not trying to manipulate them. That IS the brand proof.

---

## Visual Description

**Clean end card. Minimal.**

Same warm beige background as Scene 04. Continuity — the screen didn't change much,
just reduced further to pure product identity.

**Elements (centered, stacked):**
1. App icon — rounded square, iOS format — moderate size
2. `LoudReader` — Space Grotesk Bold, dark
3. `Free to try. No account. No tracking.` — Space Grotesk Regular, smaller
4. `App Store — search LoudReader` — smallest, neutral grey or muted

No Loud Blue on the CTA text in this scene (unlike other segments) — for this audience,
even that small embellishment might read as "trying too hard." Keep it muted.

**Animation:** Elements fade in together — no stagger, no sliding animations.
They simply appear. Quiet confidence.

---

## Audio

- **0:40–0:43:** Absolute silence. Let it breathe.
- **0:43–0:45:** A single, very quiet ambient tone fades in and holds as the ad ends.
  Not a chime — more like the sound of a room settling. Optional.

This is the only ad in this set that intentionally uses silence as a closer.
It is correct for this segment. Do not add music.

---

## Text (exact copy)

```
LoudReader
Free to try. No account. No tracking.
App Store — search LoudReader
```

## Transitions

**In:** Cut from Scene 04
**Out:** End of ad (fade to black optional, or hard cut)

## Seedance Prompt

> Minimal product end card, 16:9. Same warm beige background (#F7F5F2) as previous scene — seamless continuation. Centered: iOS app icon, app name "LoudReader" in dark bold sans-serif, one line of smaller text below, one smaller line below that. All elements appear simultaneously with a simple fade — no motion, no sliding, no animation flourishes. The entire card feels like a considered pause rather than a sales push.
