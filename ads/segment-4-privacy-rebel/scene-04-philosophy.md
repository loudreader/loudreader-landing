# Scene 04 — The Philosophy Statement
**Ad:** Segment 4 — The Privacy Rebel
**Timestamp:** 0:30–0:40
**Duration:** 10 seconds
**Format:** 16:9 horizontal
**Energy:** Still, minimal, principled — the values declaration

---

## Purpose

This is the emotional climax of the ad — not a feature list, not a demo, but a
statement of values. For the Privacy Rebel segment, how a company communicates what
it stands for matters as much as the product itself. This scene needs to feel like a
manifesto moment: quiet, confident, non-negotiable.

---

## Visual Description

**Full-screen typography only. No photography. No product UI. No people.**

Warm beige background (`#F7F5F2`) fills the entire 16:9 frame.
Text appears in the center of the screen, line by line, with pauses.

The typography is clean, modern, generous. Space Grotesk. Dark text (`#1A1A1A`).
Large enough to be read comfortably but not filling the full width — centered, breathing.

---

## Text Sequence

Each line appears with a gentle fade-in (0.3s) and holds:

```
"Your reading is yours."
```
*(pause 1.5 seconds — let it land)*

```
"Private by design."
```
*(pause 1.5 seconds)*

```
"LoudReader."
```
*(pause 1 second — the name as statement, not sales pitch)*

---

## Typography Spec

- `"Your reading is yours."` — Space Grotesk Bold, large (~48–56pt equivalent)
- `"Private by design."` — Space Grotesk Regular, slightly smaller (~36pt)
- `"LoudReader."` — Space Grotesk Bold, same size as first line OR slightly larger
  — appears in Loud Blue (`#2EC7F6`) to differentiate

No decorative elements. No icons. No underlines. Just the words.

---

## Audio

- Music: fades to nearly nothing as this scene begins — a residual pad tone only
- No voice-over — the text IS the voice
- Absolute silence is acceptable and possibly preferred for the "LoudReader." moment
- The silence is a design choice. It communicates: we don't need to fill every moment.

---

## Transitions

**In:** Dissolve from Scene 03 — the warmth of the music fading into this stillness
**Out:** Cut to Scene 05 (CTA)
