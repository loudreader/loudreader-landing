# Scene 02 — The Alternative
**Ad:** Segment 4 — The Privacy Rebel
**Timestamp:** 0:06–0:14
**Duration:** 8 seconds
**Format:** 16:9 horizontal
**Energy:** Warm, resolving, relief — the answer exists

---

## Purpose

The emotional shift. Scene 01 was cool and dark. Scene 02 is the warmth of a solution.
This is the moment of "here is the thing that's different." The visual language should
noticeably change — warmer tones, cleaner UI, breath of fresh air.

---

## Visual Description

**Full-screen → phone mockup:**

Transition from the dark of Scene 01 into a clean, centered iPhone mockup on a
soft warm background (near the LoudReader beige `#F7F5F2`).

The LoudReader app is on screen. The UI is immediately readable as calm and considered —
warm tones, clean layout, nothing surveillance-y about it. The app flows through its states
slowly enough to appreciate the design.

**Three text cards — sequential, full-screen:**

After the phone mockup establishes (~3s), three cards appear one at a time, each full-screen
centered on warm beige background:

Card 1: `No internet required`
Card 2: `No data collected`
Card 3: `Not even crash reports`

Each card: clean, centered, Space Grotesk Bold. Dark text on warm beige.
A subtle thin horizontal rule or dot separator between appearances.
Each holds for ~1.5 seconds.

---

## Lighting

Warm, soft, single-source top-light or soft box. The phone mockup is well-lit,
product-photography style. Intentionally different from Scene 01's darkness.

---

## Voice-Over

*"LoudReader processes everything on your device.*
*No servers. No analytics. No account.*
*Your books never leave your phone."*

Delivered calmly, factually. Not defensive. Confident. The tone of a company
that built something the right way and isn't apologizing for it.

---

## Audio

- Music shifts here — from low tension to a warmer, resolved ambient tone.
  Same sonic world but lighter. Think: a major chord after a minor progression.
- V/O sits cleanly over the music.

---

## Transitions

**In:** Cut from Scene 01 — the visual temperature shift should be noticeable
**Out:** Cut to Scene 03
