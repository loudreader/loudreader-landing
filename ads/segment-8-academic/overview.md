## Target Audience

**Who they are:**
PhD students, academics, researchers, professors, medical residents, lawyers,
highly educated professionals with heavy reading demands. They read not for pleasure
(though they enjoy it) but because their livelihood depends on processing large volumes
of text efficiently. They're skeptical of hype and respond to mechanism and evidence.

**Demographics:** 26–45, post-graduate educated, likely already using tools like
Zotero, Notion, Readwise. Probably listen to academic or intellectual podcasts
(Sean Carroll, Ezra Klein, 80,000 Hours).

**State of mind:** Under cognitive load. The appeal is NOT "this is easier" —
the appeal is "this is actually more effective." They won't respond to laziness framing.
Frame it as a cognitive tool that produces better outcomes.

## Why This Segment Converts

1. **Dual-coding / bimodal reading has real research support** — reading and listening
   simultaneously shows measurable comprehension and retention improvements. Lead with
   the mechanism, not the convenience.
2. **PDF import is critical** — academics deal in PDFs (papers, preprints, book chapters).
   The ability to have a paper read to them while they follow along is transformative.
3. **Offline and private** — they work in libraries, archives, conferences with bad wifi.
   Zero tracking also matters to academics who are often privacy-conscious.
4. **Speed control = efficiency** — a researcher who can process papers at 1.5×
   while following the highlighted text saves significant hours per week.
5. **Premium conversion likely** — they'll pay for a tool that genuinely improves
   their workflow, especially if they can expense it.

---

## Key Claim (research-grounded, use carefully)

The simultaneous audio + highlighted-text reading method is supported by dual-coding
theory (Paivio, 1971) and bimodal reading research showing improved comprehension
vs. reading or listening alone. This is a legitimate claim to make for this segment —
they will appreciate the intellectual honesty and look it up.
