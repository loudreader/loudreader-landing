# Scene 03 — The Workflow
**Ad:** Segment 8 — The Academic
**Timestamp:** 0:18–0:32
**Duration:** 14 seconds
**Format:** 16:9 horizontal
**Energy:** Grounded, practical, three real environments an academic actually inhabits

---

## Purpose

Show LoudReader in the actual spaces of an academic's life.
Not aspirational coffee shops — university libraries, commutes to campus, long walks
between buildings before a seminar. Real, specific, recognizable.

---

## Visual Description

Three environments, each ~4–5 seconds.

**Environment A (0:18–0:23) — University Library:**

Person at a long library desk. Large windows behind them — university aesthetic.
A printed paper or open textbook propped up in front of them.
Phone flat on the desk beside it, LoudReader open, playing.
AirPods in. They're reading the physical paper/book while the phone reads the
digital version aloud. Eyes moving between the paper and occasionally the screen.

Camera: Medium wide. The full library desk setup visible. A few other people
visible in the background, blurred.

Lighting: Classic university library — overhead fluorescents softened by large
natural windows. Cool-neutral. Studious.

**Environment B (0:23–0:27) — The Commute:**

On a train or bus, standing or seated. Academic papers on their laptop screen or
on a tablet in their lap. AirPods in. They're following text on the screen as
LoudReader reads aloud.

Their posture suggests someone used to doing serious thinking in transit —
this is not a difficult position for them to work in.

Camera: Medium, slightly over-shoulder. Train window in background.

Lighting: Natural transit light — daylight through windows, mixed with interior.

**Environment C (0:27–0:32) — Walking to a seminar:**

Outdoor campus or urban walk. Person moving between buildings, carrying a bag.
AirPods in, phone in hand, glancing at it occasionally — following the text
briefly as they walk, then looking up for the physical path.

They're using the last 10 minutes before a seminar to review a paper they need to know.

Camera: Tracking shot alongside them. Dynamic, forward movement.

Lighting: Outdoor daylight — overcast or clear. Natural.
