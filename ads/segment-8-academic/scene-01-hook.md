# Scene 01 — Hook (The Stack)
**Ad:** Segment 8 — The Academic
**Timestamp:** 0:00–0:06
**Duration:** 6 seconds
**Format:** 16:9 horizontal
**Energy:** Heavy, overwhelmed but composed — the weight of real reading demands

---

## Purpose

Show the physical reality of academic reading load. Not with drama — with precision.
The academic will look at this pile and immediately think "yes, that's mine."
The V/O adds the retention problem, which is the real anxiety.

---

## Visual Description

**Shot A (0:00–0:03) — The pile:**

Close-up of a research desk. A large stack of printed academic papers, stapled articles,
and PDF printouts. The papers have yellow highlighting visible on the top pages.
Sticky notes and annotations visible. The stack is tall — 6–8 inches.

A hand enters frame from the right and places one more paper on top.
The top paper slides slightly. The pile is near its limit.

Camera: Medium close-up, slightly overhead. Static.

**Shot B (0:03–0:06) — The person:**

Pull back to reveal the person behind the desk. PhD student or researcher —
late 20s to late 30s, glasses optional, surrounded by books and papers.
They look at the pile they just added to. Their expression is not panic — it's the
practiced composure of someone who deals with this every day.

But there's something in their eyes. Resignation, mixed with genuine curiosity —
they actually want to read all of this.

Camera: Medium shot, slight pull-back from Shot A. Natural.

---

## Lighting

Desk lamp — warm tungsten overhead but with a cooler natural daylight component
from a window (university library or home office). The kind of lighting that
says: serious work happens here.

---

## Voice-Over

*(Measured, intelligent — the register of an academic)*
*"You have more to read than hours to read it.*
*And reading alone isn't always enough to retain it."*

The second sentence lands hard for this segment. Retention is the real problem,
not just volume.

---

## Audio

- Silence until V/O begins at ~0:01
- No music in Scene 01 for this segment — let the seriousness breathe
- V/O over ambient: faint library hum or HVAC hum

## Transitions

**Out:** Cut to Scene 02 as music starts
