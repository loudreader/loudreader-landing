# Scene 03 — Everywhere
**Ad:** Segment 10 — The News Reader
**Timestamp:** 0:14–0:20
**Duration:** 6 seconds (3 cuts × 2 seconds each)
**Format:** 9:16 vertical
**Energy:** Light, satisfying, the effortless "informed life"

---

## Purpose

Show that news consumption via LoudReader happens in three moments that currently
feel incompatible with reading: morning run, cooking, commute. These aren't
productivity hacks — they're just pleasant moments made more interesting.

---

## Visual Description

**Cut A — Morning run:**

Person running outdoors. Early morning, city or park. AirPods in.
They look alert and engaged — listening to a piece of journalism, not just music.
The expression is the expression of someone who just heard something interesting
and is thinking about it while their legs run on autopilot.

Camera: Tracking from the front (reverse tracking shot), seeing their face.
Morning light — cool and clean.

**Cut B — Cooking:**

In a kitchen, early evening. Cooking dinner — stirring, chopping.
Phone propped on the counter, LoudReader app visible — an article playing.
Word-by-word highlighting visible on the screen. They're absorbing a long-form piece
while their hands are occupied.

Camera: Medium shot from the side, phone screen partially visible.
Warm kitchen lighting.

**Cut C — Walking to work:**

Person walking through a city street, commuter morning. AirPods in.
They move confidently — not rushing but purposeful. Slightly upright posture.
The look of someone who's already caught up on the news before arriving at the office.

Camera: Dynamic tracking shot from the side, city motion behind them.
Morning city light.
