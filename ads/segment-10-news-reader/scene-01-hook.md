# Scene 01 — Hook (The Graveyard)
**Ad:** Segment 10 — The News Reader
**Timestamp:** 0:00–0:04
**Duration:** 4 seconds
**Format:** 9:16 vertical
**Energy:** Ironic, self-aware, instantly recognizable — the universal read-later shame

---

## Purpose

The "read later" graveyard is universal. Every person with a smartphone has one.
This hook works because it requires zero explanation — the viewer sees it and
immediately recognizes their own behavior. The irony lands in under 2 seconds.

---

## Visual Description

**Shot type:** Extreme close-up of a smartphone screen. Screen fills the frame.

**Subject:** A "Read Later" app (Pocket, Instapaper, or a generic Safari Reading List style UI).
The list shows:
- 40+ saved articles
- Titles visible: news/long-form style ("The Future of...", "Why Everything Is...",
  "Inside the...", "What Happened When...")
- Dates visible next to articles: "3 weeks ago", "2 months ago", "5 months ago"
- One article marked: "Saved 6 months ago — 12 min read" — never touched

A finger slowly scrolls down. The list keeps going.

**Second shot (0:02–0:04):**
A different screen: an email inbox open. A newsletter — "Morning Brew", "The Atlantic",
or similar unbranded equivalent. Subject line: "This week's edition (8 unread)".
Below it: another newsletter. And another. All unread. The sender dates tell the story.

The hand closes the app with a slight resigned flick.
