# Scene 02 — The Workflow
**Ad:** Segment 10 — The News Reader
**Timestamp:** 0:04–0:14
**Duration:** 10 seconds
**Format:** 9:16 vertical
**Energy:** Practical, clear, "oh that's it?" simplicity

---

## Purpose

Show the actual workflow: save article → export as PDF → import into LoudReader → listen.
This must be shown quickly and clearly — if it looks complicated, they won't try.
The goal is to make it feel like a one-time setup that unlocks a permanent upgrade.

---

## Visual Description

**Structure:** Split-screen — left = screen recording workflow, right = person commuting.
Or: Full-frame screen recording with a real-life intercut.

**Screen recording flow (left/full screen, ~6 seconds):**

1. Safari — an article open (quality journalism style: dense headline, publication logo visible)
2. Tap the Share button (iOS)
3. "Print" → "Save as PDF" (familiar iOS flow)
4. Files app — PDF appears in the Downloads folder
5. LoudReader — Import — the article appears as a document with title and word count
6. Play button. Article text renders. Word-by-word highlighting begins.

The flow should look effortless — 6 taps from article to listening.

**Real-life intercut (right side or single cut after ~8 seconds):**

Person on a subway, earbuds in. Not looking at their phone — phone in their pocket.
A subtle audio wave indicator on their phone lock screen shows audio is playing.
They look relaxed, informed, caught up. Not scrambling, not scrolling.
