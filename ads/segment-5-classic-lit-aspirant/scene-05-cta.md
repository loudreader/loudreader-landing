# Scene 05 — CTA
**Ad:** Segment 5 — The Classic Lit Aspirant
**Timestamp:** 0:35–0:40
**Duration:** 5 seconds
**Format:** 9:16 vertical
**Energy:** Warm, elegant, unhurried — an invitation, not a command

---

## Purpose

Close gracefully. This audience responds to refinement, not urgency.
The CTA should feel like the final page of a chapter — not a pop-up ad.
The copy is aspirational ("Your library. Always with you.") rather than transactional.

---

## Visual Description

**Full-screen end card — warm, literary.**

Background: Warm beige (`#F7F5F2`) — the same tone that's run through the entire brand.
The transition from the dark bedroom of Scene 04 into this warmth should feel like
opening your eyes in the morning — gentle and right.

**Elements (centered, generous spacing):**
1. App icon — moderate size, plenty of breathing room above and below
2. `LoudReader` — Space Grotesk Bold, large
3. `Your library. Always with you.` — Space Grotesk Regular, slightly smaller.
   This tagline is chosen specifically for this segment — "your library" = the classics
   they've always aspired to, now genuinely accessible.
4. `Free on the App Store` — smallest, in Loud Blue (`#2EC7F6`)

**Animation:** Elements fade in slowly and sequentially, staggered by 0.5s each.
The whole animation takes 2.5 seconds. Holds for the remaining 2.5 seconds.

Much more spacious and slower than the student or commuter CTA cards.
This audience appreciates the unhurried pace — it signals confidence.

---

## Audio

- Single soft piano note as the icon appears — the final note of the music
- Warm silence holds for the last 2 seconds
- No voice-over

---

## Text (exact copy)

```
LoudReader
Your library. Always with you.
Free on the App Store
```

## Transitions

**In:** Soft cut from Scene 04
**Out:** Slow fade to black (optional — gives it a final cinematic feel)

> Elegant minimal product end card, 9:16 vertical. Warm soft beige background (#F7F5F2).
