## Target Audience

**Who they are:**
Intellectually curious people who have always *meant* to read the great classics.
They've had War and Peace on their shelf for three years. They tell people at dinner
they love Dostoevsky but haven't opened Crime and Punishment since college.
They love the *idea* of being a classic reader more than the actual sitting-and-reading part.

**Demographics:** 28–50, any gender, above-average income, high cultural self-image.
Likely subscribe to literary newsletters, listen to book podcasts, shop at independent
bookshops.

**State of mind when they see this ad:** Slightly wistful. Not actively looking for
a solution — but the opening image (a beautiful hardcover spine, a reading nook) will
stop them because it speaks to an aspiration they carry quietly.

---

## Emotional Arc

```
ASPIRATION (beautiful books, beautiful reading life — I want that)
  ↓
GENTLE PAIN (but life keeps filling the hours before I can start)
  ↓
REFRAME (what if those "filled hours" become the reading?)
  ↓
THE NUMBER (70,000 books... free... right now)
  ↓
INTIMACY (fall asleep inside a great story — the sleep scene)
  ↓
ACTION (this is effortless — I can start tonight, for free)
```

---

## Why This Segment Converts

1. **The free library is a category-killer hook** — 70,000 classic books at zero cost
   removes every excuse for not starting. No "I need to buy it first."
2. **The sleep-timer use case** — falling asleep to a classic novel is enormously
   appealing to this demographic. It's intimate, cozy, and aspirational.
3. **Identity fulfillment** — the purchase isn't a transaction, it's finally becoming
   the reader they always saw themselves as.
4. **Long sessions = premium conversion** — classic novel listeners tend to do long
   sessions (whole chapters). They'll want multiple voices and speed control.
5. **Instagram reach** — this aesthetic (books, cozy lighting, reading nook) performs
   extremely well on Instagram Reels to an overlapping audience.
