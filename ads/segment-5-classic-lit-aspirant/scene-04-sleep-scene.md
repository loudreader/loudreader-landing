# Scene 04 — The Sleep Scene
**Ad:** Segment 5 — The Classic Lit Aspirant
**Timestamp:** 0:28–0:35
**Duration:** 7 seconds
**Format:** 9:16 vertical
**Energy:** Intimate, warm, deeply cozy — the emotional peak

---

## Purpose

This is the most emotionally resonant scene in the entire ad. For the Classic Lit
Aspirant, the idea of falling asleep to Anna Karenina or Jane Eyre is not just
a feature — it's a fantasy. This scene delivers that fantasy completely, without
overselling it. The image does the work.

---

## Visual Description

**Shot A (0:28–0:31) — Setup:**

Person in bed. A beautiful but simple bedroom — warm, lived-in.
Bed with rumpled linen sheets in off-white or soft oat. A stack of physical books
on the bedside table (continuity with the segment's identity).
A warm amber bedside lamp is the sole light source.

The phone rests on the pillow next to their head — or is loosely in their hand,
dropping slightly. The LoudReader sleep timer UI is briefly visible on screen:
a circular countdown, numbers showing something like "12:34" ticking down.

Their eyes are still open — heavy but content. Listening to the last pages
of something they love.

Camera: Low angle, close — near pillow height. Intimate. Like sitting beside them.

**Shot B (0:31–0:35) — The falling asleep:**

Continuous from Shot A. The phone screen dims slowly (the sleep timer reaching its end —
the app auto-fades the audio). Their eyelids grow heavier. A small, satisfied expression
crosses their face — not a smile, just ease. They close their eyes.

The screen goes dark. The room is now only the amber lamp.

Camera: Stays low and close. Absolutely still.

---

## Lighting

Warm amber bedside lamp only. No overhead. No blue light from phone except
briefly at the start. As the phone dims, the scene becomes all warm amber.
This creates a visual shift from "technology-assisted" to "pure human warmth" —
the technology recedes gracefully. 2700K amber. Almost candlelight.

---

## Voice-Over

*(Very quiet — spoken gently, almost in a whisper)*
*"And fall asleep inside a great story."*

This line is delivered with genuine warmth. Not wistful, not selling — just true.
It delivers at the moment their eyes close.

---

## Audio

- Music: piano becomes single notes only — sparse, slow.
- Under the music: the AI narrator voice barely audible (mixed very low):
  *"...and she said—"* — then it fades as the screen dims.
- V/O whispered over this, then silence.
- After their eyes close: complete silence for 1 second before Scene 05 begins.

---

## Transitions

**In:** Slow dissolve from Scene 03
**Out:** Soft cut to Scene 05 after the 1-second silence
