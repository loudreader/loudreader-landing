# Scene 01 — The Dream
**Ad:** Segment 5 — The Classic Lit Aspirant
**Timestamp:** 0:00–0:05
**Duration:** 5 seconds
**Format:** 9:16 vertical (primary) / 16:9 (YouTube version)
**Energy:** Slow, beautiful, aspirational — an invitation

---

## Purpose

Seduce the viewer into the dream before telling them anything about the product.
This ad earns attention through beauty. The opening shot should feel like the first
frame of a literary film — something you want to live inside. No product, no pitch,
no text. Just the aspiration, made visual.

---

## Visual Description

**Shot A (0:00–0:02) — The book spine:**

Extreme macro close-up. A well-loved hardcover book spine fills the frame.
The title is visible: "War and Peace" (or similar canonical classic — Crime and Punishment,
Middlemarch, Les Misérables). Aged gold lettering on dark cloth binding. The texture
of the cloth is visible — slightly worn at the edges. Beautiful.

Raking warm amber light from the left catches the lettering and every fiber of the binding.
Dust motes float slowly through the beam. The rest of the frame is in deep shadow.

A single finger enters frame from the right and slowly traces the spine from bottom
to top — gently, reverentially.

Camera: Static. Macro lens. No movement. The finger does the moving.

**Shot B (0:02–0:05) — The reading nook reveal:**

Slow pull-back from the book spine. The camera reveals the full scene:
A reading corner — a leather or linen armchair beside a side table with a warm brass lamp.
More books stacked beside it. A rainy window in the background — soft grey light coming
through, rain trails on the glass. The lamp is the dominant warm light source.
This is the space every reader has imagined for themselves.

Camera: Slow dolly pull-back, revealing the space gradually. Cinematic.

---

## Lighting

Warm amber from the reading lamp (2700K). Soft grey daylight from the rainy window.
The contrast between warm lamp and cool rain light is intentional and beautiful.
No harsh shadows. Everything is gentle.

---

## Voice-Over

*"There are books you've always meant to read."*

Delivered slowly, warmly — the voice of a literary narrator, not an announcer.
Unhurried. The V/O begins at ~0:02, after the finger has appeared.

---

## Audio

- **0:00–0:02:** Silence. Or: barely audible rain ambience. Let the image speak.
- **0:02–0:05:** Soft piano begins — a single, sparse melody. Warm, minor-adjacent but
  not sad. Something from a film score, not a TV ad.
- V/O over the piano from 0:02.

---

## Transitions

**In:** Fade from black (graceful entry, not a hard cut)
**Out:** Slow dissolve to Scene 02

---

## Seedance Prompt (Shot A — Book spine)

> Extreme macro close-up, 9:16 vertical. Classic hardcover book spine — "War and Peace" in aged gold lettering on dark green or navy cloth binding. Raking warm amber light from the left reveals the cloth texture and lettering. Dust motes floating in the beam. Deep shadow everywhere else. A single elegant finger enters frame from the right and slowly traces the spine upward, reverentially. Completely static camera, macro lens, paper-thin depth of field. No background visible. Mood: literary, reverent, beautiful. Photorealistic. Film grain.

## Seedance Prompt (Shot B — Reading nook)

> Slow pull-back reveal shot, 9:16 vertical. Starting close on the book spine, pulling back to reveal a cozy reading nook: a worn leather armchair, a stack of books on a side table, a warm brass reading lamp glowing. Behind it, a large window with rain falling — soft grey natural light mixing with the warm lamp glow. The space is intimate and aspirational — a room every book lover has imagined. Slow cinematic dolly pull-back. Shallow depth of field, sharp on the armchair, soft on the rain window. Warm amber + cool grey color palette. Photorealistic. Film grain.
