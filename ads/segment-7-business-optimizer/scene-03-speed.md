# Scene 03 — The Speed Math
**Ad:** Segment 7 — The Business Optimizer
**Timestamp:** 0:18–0:30
**Duration:** 12 seconds
**Format:** 16:9 horizontal
**Energy:** Peak — this is the killer feature reveal for this segment

---

## Purpose

Speed control is the most compelling feature for the business optimizer.
The actual math — "at 2×, a business book takes 4 hours, so one book per week
on your commute" — is genuinely powerful. Show it, then prove it with numbers.
This is where the emotional peak of the ad lives for this audience.

---

## Visual Description

**Part A (0:18–0:22) — Speed demo:**

Screen recording fills frame (edge-to-edge, no phone bezel — pure screen).

LoudReader player open. A business book loaded (recognizable: "Atomic Habits" style cover,
or a whitepaper, or a business title). Speed control panel visible.

The finger drags the speed slider from 1× → 2×.
The highlighted text immediately speeds up — words flashing through lines faster.
The audio (barely audible) is clearly faster but still intelligible.

Text callout over screen: `2× speed = half the time`

**Part B (0:22–0:28) — The math:**

Clean animation on dark background (or white — high contrast).
Numbers appear line by line:

```
300-page business book:
At 1× — ~8 hours
At 2× — ~4 hours
```

*(pause)*

```
Your commute: 90 min/day
= 1 book per week
= 52 books per year
```

The final line is big, bold, and lands with visual weight.

**Part C (0:28–0:30) — The person (brief):**

Quick cut back to the person from Scene 02 — on the subway, AirPods in, satisfied nod.
They know the math. They like the math.
