# Scene 01 — Hook (The Stat)
**Ad:** Segment 7 — The Business Optimizer
**Timestamp:** 0:00–0:06
**Duration:** 6 seconds
**Format:** 16:9 horizontal
**Energy:** Bold, direct, slightly confrontational — must survive 5-second skip

---

## Purpose

Lead with the cultural mythology this audience already believes: that highly successful
people read constantly. Then immediately reframe it from aspiration into mechanism.
"That's not talent" removes the intimidation and positions LoudReader as the tool.

---

## Visual Description

**Part A (0:00–0:03) — The stat:**

Pure text on dark background — same approach as Segment 4's opening but more assertive.
Clean, centered, large:

```
The average CEO reads 60 books a year.
```

Brief pause. Then below it:
```
Are you on track?
```

No photography. No people. Just the stat. The frame is a challenge.

**Part B (0:03–0:06) — The gaps in a professional day:**

Fast cut montage — each shot half a second, no labels yet:
- Subway commute: standing, scrolling phone, wasted
- Coffee queue: waiting, phone in hand, wasted
- Gym on treadmill: looking at ceiling, listening to nothing particular
- Lunch alone: eating quickly, half-watching something

These are the gaps. Shot handheld, fast, slightly blurry — the blur of a busy life.

V/O lands over Part B:
*"That's not talent. That's just knowing when to listen."*

---

## Lighting

Part A: Pure dark/black background. White typography only.
Part B: Real-world mixed lighting — fluorescent subway, warm café, bright gym overhead,
natural lunch. Each shot authentic and unproduced.

---

## Audio

- **Part A:** Silence — the stat needs to breathe.
- **Part B:** A low, driving instrumental begins — focused, not aggressive. Think
  ambient electronica with a forward momentum. Something a founder would have on their
  focus playlist.
- V/O over Part B.

---

## Transitions

**In:** Hard cut from black (cold open)
**Out:** Hard cut to Scene 02 as music establishes

## Seedance Prompts

> **Part A:** Dark clean title card, 16:9. Near-black background. One line of large, bold white sans-serif text: "The average CEO reads 60 books a year." Below it, slightly smaller: "Are you on track?" Clean, minimal, confrontational. Space Grotesk or similar. No decoration. Motion graphics only — no photography.
