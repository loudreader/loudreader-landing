## Purpose

Close in the language of this audience. "Read like the people you admire" positions
LoudReader as a tool adopted by successful people — not sold to beginners.
The tagline is aspirational AND slightly competitive. This audience responds to both.

---

## Visual Description

Clean end card. Dark background option for this segment specifically — more aligned
with the "focused work mode" energy of the ad than the warm beige of the literary segments.

**Option A — Dark:** Near-black background. White/cyan typography.
**Option B — Clean white:** If the dark feels off-brand, use white. Both work.

**Elements:**
1. App icon — centered
2. `LoudReader` — Space Grotesk Bold
3. `Read like the people you admire.` — Space Grotesk Regular
4. `Free on the App Store` — Loud Blue (#2EC7F6)

**Animation:** Clean, confident, simultaneous fade-in. Not staggered — these people
don't have time for flourishes.
