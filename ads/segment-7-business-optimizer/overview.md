## Target Audience

**Who they are:**
Entrepreneurs, startup founders, VCs, executives, managers climbing fast.
They've read (or want to read) all the canonical business/self-dev books —
Zero to One, Thinking Fast and Slow, Atomic Habits, The Hard Thing About Hard Things.
They measure everything in ROI and treat time as their scarcest resource.
They listen to How I Built This, Lex Fridman, My First Million.

**Demographics:** 25–45, male-skewing but not exclusively, high income, highly goal-oriented.
LinkedIn active. Follow founders and VCs on Twitter/X.

**State of mind:** Always optimizing. They will see an efficiency tool and their
instinct is "how do I add this to my stack?" Not emotional buyers — rational ones
who respond to numbers and peer behaviour.

## Why This Segment Converts

1. **The "CEOs read X books a year" hook** — this is already part of their cultural
   mythology. LoudReader makes that number achievable for the first time.
2. **Speed control = genuine productivity multiplier** — a business book at 2×
   is completed in 3–4 hours instead of 6–8. That's a measurable return.
3. **High disposable income** — will absolutely pay premium without hesitation.
4. **Import any book** — they'll import PDFs of whitepapers, reports, business
   books they already own. Immediate utility, zero new purchase needed.
5. **LinkedIn-native sharing** — this segment shares tools that work. One LinkedIn
   post with "I've been using this to get through 3–4 business books a month" = viral.
