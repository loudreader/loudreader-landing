# Scene 02 — The Stack (Day in the Life)
**Ad:** Segment 7 — The Business Optimizer
**Timestamp:** 0:06–0:18
**Duration:** 12 seconds
**Format:** 16:9 horizontal
**Energy:** Efficient, aspirational, the "high performer's day" fantasy

---

## Purpose

Show this person's life — optimized. Every dead moment in their schedule is now
productive. The timestamps make this feel like a system, not a lifestyle. For the
business optimizer, a system beats inspiration every time.

---

## Visual Description

Four shots, each with a timestamp — evoking a calendar, a schedule, an optimized day.

**Shot A — 6:30 AM / Treadmill Run:**
Person on a treadmill, running at a strong pace. AirPods in. Phone mounted to the
treadmill display or in a sport armband. LoudReader visible — a business book loaded,
text highlighting. They look sharp, focused — not just working out, processing.
Clean modern gym. Early morning light through windows.
Timestamp: `6:30 AM`

**Shot B — 8:00 AM / Subway Commute:**
Same professional on a subway, standing, holding a rail. AirPods in, eyes slightly
unfocused — the look of someone actively thinking, not zoning out.
Phone in their other hand, screen faintly visible.
Timestamp: `8:00 AM`

**Shot C — 12:30 PM / Lunch:**
Person eating at their desk alone, or at a café. Simple quick lunch. AirPods in,
laptop closed. This is deliberate — they're not multitasking with screens.
They're using lunch to listen and think. Notepad nearby.
Timestamp: `12:30 PM`

**Shot D — 10:00 PM / Wind Down:**
Getting ready for bed — face wash, brushing teeth, moving around the apartment.
AirPods in. Still going. The day's last chapter.
Timestamp: `10:00 PM`

Each shot: ~3 seconds. Cinematic, warm, aspirational. This is the life they want.
