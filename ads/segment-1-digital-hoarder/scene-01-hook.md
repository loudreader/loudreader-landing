## Purpose

Stop the scroll. The viewer must see themselves immediately. No intro, no build-up —
the first frame IS the joke. This is the "pattern interrupt" that earns the next 26 seconds.

## Visual Description

**Shot type:** Extreme close-up. The phone screen fills 80% of the frame.

**Subject:** A smartphone (iPhone, modern, no brand logo clearly shown) held in one hand.
The screen displays a reading app library — a dense, colorful grid of ebook covers
stretching far below the visible area. Classic book covers, self-help titles, sci-fi,
non-fiction — a mix suggesting years of impulse purchases. They all look unread (no
progress bars, or all at 0%).

**Key action:** A thumb slowly scrolls downward. The grid keeps going. And going.
There is a tiny, barely perceptible sigh energy to the motion — not dramatic, just tired.

**Background:** Softly out-of-focus domestic interior. A couch in the background.
Warm, dim, indoor evening light. The bright phone screen is the clear focal point.

**Camera movement:** Static. Locked off. The only motion is the scrolling thumb and the
grid moving upward.
