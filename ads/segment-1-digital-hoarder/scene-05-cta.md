# Scene 05 — CTA
**Ad:** Segment 1 — The Digital Hoarder
**Timestamp:** 0:27–0:30
**Duration:** 3 seconds
**Format:** 9:16 vertical
**Energy:** Clean, clear, confident

---

## Purpose

Convert curiosity into action. The viewer has been entertained and has seen the product.
Now give them exactly one thing to do. No clutter. No second message.

---

## Visual Description

**Shot type:** Full-frame graphic / title card — not live action.

**Background:** Soft warm beige (`#F7F5F2`) fills the entire frame. Clean, minimal.
This is the same background tone as the LoudReader app UI — brand consistency.

**Elements (centered, stacked vertically):**
1. App icon — large, centered, top half of frame
2. "LoudReader" — logotype in Space Grotesk Bold below the icon
3. Tagline — "Your books. Finally heard." in Space Grotesk Regular, smaller
4. CTA line — "App Store ↓" in Loud Blue (#2EC7F6), smallest, bottom

**Animation:** Each element fades or slides up into place, staggered by 0.3s.
Total animation takes ~1.5 seconds. Holds for the remaining 1.5 seconds.
