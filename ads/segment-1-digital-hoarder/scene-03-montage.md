# Scene 03 — The Montage
**Ad:** Segment 1 — The Digital Hoarder
**Timestamp:** 0:10–0:22
**Duration:** 12 seconds total (4 cuts × ~3 seconds each)
**Format:** 9:16 vertical
**Energy:** Upbeat, kinetic, freeing

---

## Purpose

Show the product living in the real world. Each cut answers the unspoken question:
"okay but when would I actually use this?" The answer is: everywhere, all the time,
during moments you're already living.

---

## Cut A — The Metro (0:10–0:13)

**Visual:** Person walking purposefully through a busy subway or metro station.
Rush-hour energy — commuters around them, blurred motion in background.
Our person has AirPods in and moves with a slightly elevated, unhurried confidence
compared to the stressed crowd around them. Phone visible in jacket pocket, screen
glowing slightly (app playing). Small, private smile on their face.

**Camera:** Dynamic tracking shot — camera follows at chest level, slightly low angle,
moving with the subject. Slight motion blur on background commuters.

**Lighting:** Harsh metro fluorescent overhead light. High contrast. Slightly cold.
Feels real, not staged.

**Text overlay:** None

**Audio:** Music kicks in here — lo-fi upbeat instrumental. Energetic but not cheesy.
Underneath: faint AI narrator voice (barely audible — they're the ones hearing it).

**Seedance prompt for this cut:**
> Dynamic tracking shot, 9:16 vertical, following a young adult walking confidently through a busy underground metro station at rush hour. They have white AirPods in, a small relaxed smile. Other commuters blur past in the background. Harsh fluorescent overhead lighting, realistic subway environment. Handheld tracking camera at chest height, slight motion blur. Cinematic. Photorealistic. Film grain.

## Cut B — The Kitchen (0:13–0:16)

**Visual:** Same person (or different — continuity not required in montage),
cooking in a modern kitchen. Steam rising from a pot on the stove.
They're chopping vegetables on a cutting board. Phone propped against a
glass or small stand on the counter — the LoudReader app visible,
text scrolling and highlighting. They're clearly listening, slightly nodding.
Completely hands-free engagement.

**Camera:** Medium wide, slightly overhead angle to show phone on counter.
Static or very slow push in. Warm kitchen lighting.

**Lighting:** Warm kitchen light — pendant lamps above island, late afternoon sun
through a window. Golden hour feel. Domestic and inviting.

**Text overlay:** None

**Audio:** Continuation of the lo-fi beat. No voice-over.

**Seedance prompt for this cut:**
> Medium shot, 9:16 vertical. Person cooking in a modern home kitchen — chopping vegetables, a pot of water steaming on the stove behind them. A smartphone is propped against a glass on the counter showing a reading/listening app with text highlighted in cyan. Warm golden pendant kitchen lighting. Late afternoon sun through a window. Comfortable, domestic, aspirational. Slow push-in camera. Shallow depth of field on the phone screen. Photorealistic. Film grain.

## Cut C — The Plane (0:16–0:19)

**Visual:** Window seat on an airplane. Person has their phone in their lap or on the
tray table. App is playing. The airplane mode icon is clearly visible in the top-right
corner of the phone screen. Clouds visible through the oval window behind them.
They look relaxed — not the anxious traveler — they're content, absorbed.

**Camera:** Close-medium shot, slightly over-the-shoulder to show both the person
and the phone screen. Static. The window and clouds visible in the background.

**Lighting:** Diffused natural window light from the airplane window. Soft and clean.
Slight cool tone from the altitude.

**Text overlay:** `No wifi? Doesn't matter.`
**Style:** White, small, elegant — lower third. Appears at 0:17, fades by end of cut.
**Font:** Space Grotesk Medium

**Audio:** Continuation of the beat. Subtle airplane ambient hum underneath.

**Seedance prompt for this cut:**
> Interior airplane window seat, 9:16 vertical. Person sitting relaxed in economy or business class, phone in lap showing a reading app with cyan word highlighting. Airplane mode icon visible top-right corner of phone screen. Oval airplane window behind them shows white clouds. Soft diffused natural light from window. Over-the-shoulder angle showing both person and phone. Calm, comfortable, peaceful. Static camera. Cinematic. Photorealistic.

## Cut D — The Morning Run (0:19–0:22)

**Visual:** Outdoor early morning run. City skyline or park path in the background.
Golden-pink pre-dawn or early morning light (6–7am feel). Person running at a steady pace,
AirPods in, relaxed running form. They look like someone who has their life together —
not exhausted, genuinely enjoying it. The listening is what's making the run enjoyable.

**Camera:** Slightly ahead of them, reverse tracking shot so we see their face. Or a
side-tracking shot. Slight natural motion shake. Dynamic.

**Lighting:** Golden hour / blue hour morning. Warm pinks and oranges in the sky.
Long shadows. Beautiful.

**Text overlay:** None (let the beauty of the shot close the montage)

**Audio:** Music peaks slightly here — the most energetic moment of the spot.
Then begins to wind down leading into Scene 04.

**Seedance prompt for this cut:**
> Outdoor urban running scene, 9:16 vertical, golden early morning light. Person running at a relaxed pace on a city path or sidewalk, city skyline or park in background. They have white earbuds in, relaxed confident expression — not struggling, genuinely enjoying the run. Pink-orange dawn sky, long shadows. Dynamic reverse tracking shot showing their face. Slight handheld motion. Cinematic warm color grade. Photorealistic. Film grain.
