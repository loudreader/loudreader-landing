## Target Audience

**Who they are:**
People who've accumulated a large library of ebooks — Kindle purchases, Apple Books, Humble Bundle PDFs, DRM-free downloads — that they've never actually read. The purchase felt good. The reading never happened. The guilt is real and recurring.

**Demographics:** 25–40, any gender, moderate-to-high disposable income, identifies as a "reader" even though they rarely read.

**Platforms they're on:** TikTok (relatable content), YouTube Shorts, Reddit (r/books, r/kindle).

**State of mind when they see this ad:** Scrolling passively, probably avoiding something. The opening visual stops them because it mirrors their exact reality.

---

## Emotional Arc

```
GUILT (I have all these books I've never touched)
  ↓
RECOGNITION (oh that's exactly me)
  ↓
REVELATION (wait, I can just... listen to them?)
  ↓
RELIEF (I don't have to start from zero)
  ↓
ACTION (free to try → download)
```

---

## Why This Segment Converts

1. **Zero barrier to entry** — they already have the files. No new purchase decision needed.
2. **Existing guilt is a pre-sold pain point** — problem is obvious the moment they see scene 1.
3. **Identity preservation** — they still get to call themselves readers.
4. **Frictionless CTA** — "free to try" removes the final hesitation.
