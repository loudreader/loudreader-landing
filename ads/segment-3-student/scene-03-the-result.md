# Scene 03 — The Result
**Ad:** Segment 3 — The Student
**Timestamp:** 0:08–0:14
**Duration:** 6 seconds
**Format:** 9:16 vertical
**Energy:** Confident, satisfied, proof delivered

---

## Purpose

Show the outcome, not the process. The viewer needs to believe 200 pages in 1.5 hours
is real. The visual proof is a student who looks like they *won* — not exhausted,
not cramming — genuinely engaged, taking intelligent notes, ahead of the game.

---

## Visual Description

**Part A (0:08–0:11) — Student at desk:**

Same student as Scene 02 (or visually consistent). Now at a proper desk or café table.
AirPods in. Notepad open beside them — they're writing notes by hand, actively.
Their posture is open, leaning slightly forward. Not the slumped, defeated study
posture — this is someone in flow state.

Phone propped against a water bottle or textbook, app visible and running.
They occasionally glance at the phone screen where words are still highlighting —
then write something down. Eyes moving between screen and notepad.

Camera: Medium shot, slightly off-center. Real, natural. Natural window light.

**Part B (0:11–0:14) — Close-up: hand writing notes:**

Cut to close-up of the notepad. The hand is writing fast — confident strokes,
abbreviations, key concepts being captured. The pen moves quickly, efficiently.
This is someone who understood what they heard and is capturing it.

Camera: Tight close-up from above, slightly angled. The page should be partially legible
but doesn't need to be specifically readable — just conveys active, efficient note-taking.
