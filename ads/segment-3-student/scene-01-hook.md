## Purpose

Instant pattern interrupt. Every student who sees this will recognize the exact
physical sensation of looking at a book you have to read tonight. The slow zoom
in on the dense pages is visceral. The caption does the rest.

## Visual Description

**Shot type:** POV — looking downward at a desk, as if through the student's own eyes.

**Subject:** A thick, dense book lying open on a wooden desk or table.
The book should look genuinely intimidating — tight typeset, small margins, dense text.
Good choices: Dostoyevsky (The Brothers Karamazov, Crime and Punishment), an economics
textbook, a philosophy reader, a canonical novel from an English lit syllabus.
The book is open but untouched — spine flat, pages undisturbed.

**Key motion:** The camera very slowly zooms in toward the open pages —
as if you're leaning forward to confront it but not quite ready.
Creeping dread made visual.

**Environment:** Study desk. Late evening or night. Cluttered but not chaotic —
a highlighter, maybe a coffee cup, earbuds coiled nearby. Relatable student desk energy.

**Hand:** One hand partially visible at the bottom of frame, resting on the desk
near the book but not touching it. Hesitant. This is someone steeling themselves.
