# Scene 01 — Hook (The Loved Book)
**Ad:** Segment 6 — The Avid Reader
**Timestamp:** 0:00–0:05
**Duration:** 5 seconds
**Format:** 9:16 vertical
**Energy:** Intimate, beautiful, slow — deep recognition

---

## Purpose

Stop an avid reader with an image they recognize as theirs.
A heavily annotated, genuinely loved book is an identity object for this group.
No copy needed for the first 2 seconds — the image speaks directly to them.

---

## Visual Description

**Shot type:** Slow panning macro close-up.

**Subject:** A well-loved paperback or hardcover, open to a page mid-book.
The page has: pencil underlining, margin notes in small handwriting, a star next to
a paragraph, a few words circled. Multiple sticky-tab bookmarks visible at the top edge.
The spine is cracked from repeated opening. The pages are slightly yellowed or cream.

The camera pans slowly left-to-right across the open page — not reading it,
but appreciating it. The way you'd look at a painting.

A hand enters from below and gently turns to the next page.

**Background:** Completely soft bokeh — warm amber tones (a reading chair,
a lamp, wooden surface). The book is the only thing in focus.

---

## Lighting

Warm amber raking light from the left (reading lamp direction).
The light catches the indentations of pencil pressure in the page.
The handwritten annotations cast tiny shadows.
Beautiful, warm, golden. No harsh shadows.

---

## Voice-Over

*(Warm, conversational — the voice of someone who also reads)*
*"You already love books."*

Delivered at 0:02 — after the viewer has had 2 seconds to register the image.

---

## Audio

- **0:00–0:02:** Silence. The crinkle of the page turning is the only sound.
- **0:02–0:05:** Single warm piano note. V/O over it.

---

## Transitions

**In:** Fade from black — gentle entry
**Out:** Cut to Scene 02
