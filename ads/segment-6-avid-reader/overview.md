## Target Audience

**Who they are:**
People who already read voraciously — 20–50 books a year. They have Goodreads accounts,
follow #bookstagram, dog-ear pages, own multiple reading lights. They don't need to be
convinced reading is good. They need to see LoudReader as an upgrade, not a replacement.

Their frustration: they want to read MORE but keep hitting the same physical constraint —
you can only read while your eyes and hands are free and your mind is at rest.
Walking, cooking, exercising — all reading dead zones. LoudReader removes those constraints.

**Demographics:** 22–45, any gender, high book purchase rate, will absolutely upgrade
to premium for better voices and speed.

**State of mind:** Probably on Instagram or YouTube looking at book content —
BookTok adjacent. The opening frame of a beautifully marked-up book with sticky tabs
will stop them cold.


---

## Why This Segment Converts

1. **No behavior change needed** — they already love reading. This just extends when.
2. **Premium conversion is very high** — avid readers will pay for better voices and
   speed control. They're already spending money on books.
3. **Word highlighting is a feature they specifically love** — the visual reading
   experience isn't lost. It's just also audio. That matters to this group.
4. **The 70k free library** — they may have already read many of these classics,
   but re-listening is a real use case. Or filling in the gaps they never got to.
5. **BookTok/Bookstagram community** — this audience shares what they use.
   One authentic creator recommendation can generate thousands of installs.
