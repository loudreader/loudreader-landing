# Scene 02 — The Gap
**Ad:** Segment 6 — The Avid Reader
**Timestamp:** 0:05–0:13
**Duration:** 8 seconds
**Format:** 9:16 vertical
**Energy:** Punchy, rhythmic, slightly frustrating → immediately resolved

---

## Purpose

Show all the moments in a reader's day where they physically cannot read —
then immediately flip each one. The ✗ → ✓ transformation should feel satisfying
and fast. This scene creates the need and the solution in the same breath.

---

## Visual Description

**Structure:** 4 quick pairs of shots (2 seconds each).
Each pair: [Moment where reading is impossible] → [Same moment with LoudReader].

**Pair 1 — Dog walk:**
- BEFORE: Person walking a dog, one hand on the leash, one hand in pocket.
  Can't hold a book. Text overlay: `commuting ✗`
- AFTER: Same walk, AirPods in, small smile. Text overlay: `commuting ✓`

**Pair 2 — Cooking:**
- BEFORE: Stirring a pot, two hands busy. A book is open on the counter but
  they can't read and stir simultaneously. Text overlay: `cooking ✗`
- AFTER: Stirring the pot, AirPods in, relaxed. Text overlay: `cooking ✓`

**Pair 3 — Gym / exercise:**
- BEFORE: On a treadmill, speed up, can't focus on a book. Text overlay: `working out ✗`
- AFTER: Same treadmill, AirPods in, engaged expression. Text overlay: `working out ✓`

**Pair 4 — Chores:**
- BEFORE: Folding laundry, distracted, slightly bored. Text overlay: `chores ✗`
- AFTER: Same chores, AirPods in, visibly happier. Text overlay: `chores ✓`

Camera: Each shot is 1 second, handheld or static. Fast cuts. The contrast is the edit.

---

## Lighting

Natural, varied per location. Keep each shot feeling real and unproduced.
Dog walk: outdoor daylight. Kitchen: warm pendant light. Gym: bright overhead.
Laundry: neutral indoor.

---

## Text Overlays

Each label appears instantly at the start of each shot:
- `commuting ✗` / `commuting ✓`
- `cooking ✗` / `cooking ✓`
- `working out ✗` / `working out ✓`
- `chores ✗` / `chores ✓`

**Style:** White, TikTok-caption style. The ✗ is red-tinted; the ✓ is cyan (#2EC7F6).

---

## Voice-Over

*(Over the whole sequence, paced briskly)*
*"You could be reading in all of these moments."*

Delivered to land just as the final ✓ appears.

---

## Audio

The rhythm of the beat matches the cut pace.

## Transitions

**Out:** Hard cut to Scene 03
