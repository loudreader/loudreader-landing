# Scene 04 — More Books
**Ad:** Segment 6 — The Avid Reader
**Timestamp:** 0:24–0:30
**Duration:** 6 seconds
**Format:** 9:16 vertical
**Energy:** Aspirational, satisfying, slightly exciting

---

## Purpose

Land the core promise for this segment specifically: not "start reading" but
"read significantly more." The number reframe (30 → 60 books) should feel
attainable and exciting — not an exaggeration. Close with a physical bookshelf
moment that speaks directly to their identity.

---

## Visual Description

**Part A (0:24–0:27) — Typography animation:**

Full-screen text on warm beige (#F7F5F2).
Text appears line by line with slight stagger:

```
You read 30 books a year.
```
*(pause 0.5s)*
```
Imagine 60.
```

The word "Imagine" is in regular weight; "60." is bold and large.
The number feels like a dare, not a boast.

**Part B (0:27–0:30) — Bookshelf moment:**

Person (the same reader from Scene 03) standing in front of a personal bookshelf —
well-organized, full, clearly curated over years. Their collection.
They run a finger slowly along the spines from left to right.
A small, private, satisfied smile.

Camera: Medium shot, slightly low angle so the bookshelf fills the background.
The books behind them are slightly soft, the person is sharp.

---

## Lighting

Part B: Warm room light — same reading environment. A bookshelf well-lit by natural
window light or a nearby warm lamp. The spines of the books catch the light.

None in this scene — the typography and image carry it.
