## Purpose

The most important scene for this segment specifically. Avid readers may feel that
listening to a book "doesn't count" as reading. This scene directly addresses that
anxiety by showing the word-by-word highlighting — which makes it genuinely
a visual + auditory reading experience. The copy reinforces it explicitly.

---

## Visual Description

**Part A (0:13–0:18) — Screen recording close-up:**

Full-frame screen recording of the LoudReader player.
A literary book is loaded (not a thriller — something like Middlemarch, To the Lighthouse,
or a short story collection — something that signals "real" reading to this audience).

The text is displayed cleanly on the warm beige screen.
The AI narrator reads steadily at 1× or 1.2× — not rushed.
Each word highlights in cyan (#2EC7F6) as it's spoken, then returns to dark.
The effect is beautiful — like a spotlight moving through sentences.

The camera slowly pushes in on the screen, so the highlighted text fills more
and more of the frame. Close enough that we can appreciate the individual word glow.

**Part B (0:18–0:24) — Person reading along:**

Person sitting somewhere comfortable (reading nook, café, back garden).
Phone propped or held. AirPods in. But they are actively following the text —
eyes moving across the screen, tracking the highlighted words. Nodding slightly.
This is not passive listening. They are reading.

Camera: Medium close-up. The phone screen is partially visible. Their eyes move.
